# muse-excusealmost-entry

Entry for the make.fun competition [The Excuse That Almost Works](https://make.fun/bounty/u-clever-harbor-75-the-excuse-that-almost-works-69fde8f5).

## The entry

> sorry, flat's boiler died, plumber only has today, I'm stood in two inches of water in my socks

Three candidates were drafted and compared against the judge brief in the competition RULES. Selection reason: the most mundane and specific failure (a dead boiler, a plumber with only today, two inches of water and wet socks) reads like a real hurried text a tired manager would almost believe, and the laugh comes from the detail itself.

## How it works

`main.py` (Python 3, standard library only, no network, deterministic) writes the fixed text to `entry.txt` (entry.txt sha256 b73ae8aa703b73a16ceec043a89ff15af07647fff4752b78f75d4bb882c8aa15).
